# Community guidelines

Welcome learners, researchers, educators and developers with different backgrounds.
Keep discussions constructive, credit the work of others, and critique claims using
evidence. Report failures candidly and make room for corrections.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory attacks, deliberate exposure of personal information
and attempts to identify private people through this project are not welcome. Use
fictional data or recordings with documented permission. Do not post credentials or
private locations in issues, discussions or pull requests.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions or restrict participation
when necessary to keep the project useful and respectful. If a post exposes personal
information, use GitHub's reporting tools and ask a maintainer to remove it without
reposting that information.

These participation guidelines do not change the MIT software license.
